How to play
  • You have 3 hearts ❤️. A wrong answer costs a heart. Lose them all and the run is over.
  • 3 right in a row → LEVEL UP! (harder scenarios + 1 bonus heart, up to 5).
  • 2 wrong in a row → warm-up: you drop one level to practise easier ones.
  • Streaks multiply your points: ×2 from the 4th in a row, ×3 from the 7th, ×4 from the 10th.
  • Question types: build an FBD, pick the right FBD, spot the fake force, predict the motion, calculate, concept questions and 3rd-law matching.
  • Keys: 1–4 pick an answer, Enter checks / goes on.
