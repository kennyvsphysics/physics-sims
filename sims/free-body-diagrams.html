How to use this simulator (students & teacher notes)
  • Pick a scenario at the top, or a “Try:” preset. Every number can be changed in Settings.
  • ▶ Run animates the motion using Newton’s 2nd law, Fnet = ma. Force arrows on the object are drawn to scale (longer = bigger force) from the object’s center. The dashed gray arrow is the net force, which is the sum of the forces, not another force.
  • The free-body diagram panel shows only the object as a dot, with every force acting on it.
  • Predict mode: the arrows and numbers are hidden. Build your own free-body diagram for the moment shown (you can pause a run partway and predict at that moment). Pick what the object will do, then press Check. The sim tells you about missing forces, extra forces that don’t exist (like a “force of motion”), wrong directions, and arrows that are the wrong size compared to each other. Reveal shows the correct diagram over yours (yours dashed) and runs the motion.
  • Level: “Regents” hides angled pushes, components, tilted axes, two-rope and pulley problems. “AP Physics 1” shows all of them plus the static friction threshold μsFN.
  • Graphs show velocity, acceleration, and net force vs. time along the direction of motion (signs: see the + direction under each scenario). The data table records t, position, v, a, Fnet and every force while it runs. Download CSV opens in Google Sheets or Excel.
  • Physics used: Fg = mg (g = 9.8 m/s² by default). Static friction matches whatever is needed up to μsFN. Once the object slides, kinetic friction is μkFN opposite the sliding. Air resistance is Fair = kv². In the elevator the scale reads FN = m(g + a).
